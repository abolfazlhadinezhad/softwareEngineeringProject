# Privacy, retention and institutional decisions

No national-ID fields, default national-ID passwords, public registration, real student data or external university integration are implemented. Synthetic seed accounts are development-only and require an operator-supplied random password. Imported students are inactive and have unusable passwords until an expiring high-entropy token is distributed by an authorized university mechanism and the student chooses a validated password. Activation tokens are stored only as SHA-256 digests, sent in request bodies and excluded from audits.

Private files have UUID storage names, extension/MIME/signature/size validation and authorized attachment downloads. Nginx has no private-media route. Downloads use attachment disposition and nosniff. PDF/PNG/JPEG/WebP are supported; Office documents, antivirus quarantine and S3 adapters are not enabled. Signature validation is not malware scanning. Backups must be treated as confidential student data and encrypted with restricted access.

Sessions are Django database sessions with HTTP-only cookies, SameSite Lax and production Secure/HTTPS defaults. Login, activation and anonymous access are throttled through Redis; strong password validation and current-password verification protect password changes. Password change invalidates prior sessions. MFA, general password-reset delivery, logout-all control, account-specific brute-force locks, reauthentication and institution SSO remain unimplemented; do not enroll real students before these identity requirements are reviewed.

Before deployment, university stakeholders must approve account eligibility and token distribution, department hierarchy and independent complaint reviewers, public contact/branding assets, business calendars/SLA targets, retention periods for tickets/files/import previews/audits/backups, access-grant approvals, external delivery providers, data subject requests and incident response responsibilities. The displayed privacy text is provisional. No legal/security compliance claim is made.

No automatic deletion policy is active. Import previews currently persist in MySQL (commit validity 24 hours); periodic secure purge and audit archival must be implemented after the retention schedule is approved. Restrict DB administrative access; keep encrypted off-site backups; record access to restored datasets. Audit APIs are read-only but database administrators can alter records; cryptographic or external append-only audit storage is future work.
